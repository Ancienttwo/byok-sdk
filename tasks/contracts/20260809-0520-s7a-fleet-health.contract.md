## Why

S7 RC 必须证明 fleet reconnect peak 有界，并让 doctor/support bundle 读取真实 health/crash authority。当前 WS 唯一 jitter 来自 `Math.random()`，long-poll/outbox/maintenance 仍用固定 cadence；同一故障无法复现，也无法证明大批 device 不聚簇。现有 `degraded` 只是 transport fallback，不记录 daemon operational failure window 或上次进程是否异常退出。

## Goal

交付 client-only deterministic fleet timing 与 local operational health authority：以已加载的 product/device identity 做稳定 seed，按 reconnect/upload/maintenance domain 分离自动 retry；显式 operator retry 不延迟。持久化 60s sliding failure window、3 次 failure degraded、recovering/healthy 转移、unclean-run marker 与 bounded crash history，并通过 daemon/control/CLI status 诚实投影。

## Scope

- In scope: deterministic jitter production primitive；WS/long-poll/outbox/maintenance automatic retry wiring；fleet simulation；operational health state/run marker/crash history；daemon/control/CLI status projection；architecture/sprint/notes sync。
- Out of scope: doctor/support bundle UI、quarantine cleanup、wire/presence schema、cloud/server/core/keys behavior、SQL migration、npm identity/publish。
- Taste constraints: one timing authority；no random fallback；no reuse of wire connection/presence enum；corrupt health state reports typed unavailable and is not deleted/rebuilt；no secret/prompt bytes in health state。

## Stop Conditions

- Stop if identity is unavailable at every automatic retry construction point。
- Stop if manual/operator retry cannot remain immediate。
- Stop if health/crash evidence requires protocol、schema or migration changes。
- Stop if full hard-dataplane gates cannot run。

## Falsifier

最便宜 proof：对同一 `deviceId/domain/attempt` 重算 100 次必须得到相同 delay；对 10,000 个 device 的同一 attempt 做 bucket simulation，peak 必须低于无 jitter cohort 的明确上限。若任一 automatic retry 仍读 `Math.random()`/固定 shared interval，或 clean shutdown 下次启动被记 crash，方向即证伪。
