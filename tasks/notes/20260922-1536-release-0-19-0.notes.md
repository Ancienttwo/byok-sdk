## Design Decisions

- **Two authorities, three physical copies.** `packages/core/package.json` and `packages/keys/package.json` are the only version authorities; the ten aligned manifests, the eleven `bun.lock` workspace records, and `README.md` + `docs/spec.md` are all projections of them. The bump therefore moved the authorities and then made every projection agree, rather than editing any document to a number of its own.
- **`bun.lock` is hand-edited.** `bun install` does not rewrite the workspace `version` records on a version-only bump, and `bun pm pack` resolves `workspace:*` from those records rather than from the manifests. The eleven records were edited directly; `bun install --frozen-lockfile` afterwards reported "no changes", and `check:release-pack` proved the packed edges: every tarball's internal `@byok-sdk` edges pin `0.19.0`, including `byok-sdk-keys-0.6.0.tgz`.
- **`v0.18.0-handoff.md` gets a correction note, not a rewrite.** It is the historical preparation receipt for a train that has since been published; rewriting its body would erase the evidence that the repo drifted from the registry. A dated block at the top states the current fact and links the 0.19.0 handoff; the body keeps its original wording with the status line marked "as written, 2026-09-11".
- **The `CHANGELOG.md` 0.18.0 heading was corrected too.** It read "unpublished release candidate" for a train that is `latest` on npm. The section body is untouched.
- **No fresh empty `## Unreleased` heading.** The file's own precedent is the 0.18.0 cut (`aa049307`), which renamed the top section in place and left no empty Unreleased behind; this cut follows it.
- **keys takes a MINOR by the policy, not by preference.** `docs/spec.md#pre-10-package-version-policy` makes MINOR cover "any pre-1.0 breaking cut", and `--runtime-entry {pi-rpc, pi-prepared}` is now required at the launcher parser. `check:version-authority` and `check:release-graph` do not force a specific number — they only require keys ≠ the train version — so the number is a human decision and is flagged for the Owner in the handoff and the PR body.
