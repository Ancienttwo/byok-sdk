## Fixed

### Claude and Codex login probes under a macOS launchd service

On 0.46.0, `ClaudeAdapter` and `CodexAdapter` bounded every detect probe at
5 s. Inside a macOS launchd service under host load,
`claude auth status --json` was measured at 18 s, so the probe was killed and
a signed-in Claude was reported as signed out. Every task offer was declined
non-retryably. [Refs #2468](https://github.com/Ancienttwo/byok-sdk/issues/2468)

0.46.1 changes four things (PR #2470):

- **A probe deadline is no verdict.** A login-status probe that reaches its
  deadline reports `detect()` `{ kind: 'timeout' }` (retryable
  `probe_timeout`) instead of `authPresent: false`. A Claude subscription
  launch whose login read times out fails with a retryable start failure
  instead of the non-retryable subscription-required error. A Codex
  `app-server --help` deadline reports `timeout` instead of
  `refused: app_server_unavailable`, and a Codex `prepare()` that hits a probe
  deadline rejects retryably. A confirmed sign-out, a crash, or unreadable
  output keeps its earlier result.
- **Split detect budgets.** `--version` and Codex `app-server --help` allow
  10 s. Claude `auth status --json`, Codex `login status`, and the Codex
  subscription account read allow 25 s. On deadline the probe closes the
  child's pipes and kills it with `SIGKILL`, so a hung CLI still cannot block
  daemon startup or an offer.
- **Admission reads the login once.** Task admission never reads
  `authPresent`, so the built-in Claude and Codex adapters skip the login probe
  during admission. A subscription launch still reads the login in its own
  launch context. A custom adapter, a spied `detect`, or a subclass override
  keeps its own `detect()` path.
- **`ProcessType=Interactive` for launchd.** The rendered launchd plist sets
  `<key>ProcessType</key><string>Interactive</string>`. Without it macOS runs
  the daemon and the runtime CLIs it spawns at throttled `PRI = 20`, which
  starved the keychain reads under load.

`byok-agent runtimes` and offline `doctor` now allow 35 s per runtime probe
(from 5 s), which covers Claude's worst case of 10 s version plus 25 s login
status. At the deadline the probe aborts the `AbortSignal` passed to
`detect(signal)`; an adapter that ignores the signal is still reported as
`timeout`.

## Residual boundary

An existing launchd plist written by 0.46.0 or earlier lacks `ProcessType`.
`service-status` reports it as `repairable`. Run `install()` or `repair()` on
0.46.1 to rewrite the plist; until then the service keeps the throttled
priority, and only the longer budgets and the timeout classification apply.
A login read slower than 25 s still reports `timeout` and is retried rather
than admitted.
