### Contract decisions to implement

- **O1/O2:** one log row per admitted source envelope: plaintext progress batch, opaque sealed progress batch, or plaintext artifact. Store-assigned revision is arrival order; `batchSeq` and `eventIndex` remain provenance/display metadata. A progress batch is one retained log entry even when the union reader emits several agent events. Plaintext artifact payloads are recorded once; no relay artifact buffer. Sealed artifacts are explicitly excluded from log admission until #1045 item 6 is delivered. Existing sealed-artifact blob handling is not silently replaced or represented as successful log persistence. Do not expand embedded-server sealing admission in this slice.
- **O3:** at most 1,000 retained payload entries and 4 MiB of serialized UTF-8 entry bytes per task, including metadata and ciphertext encoding. Document the exact serialization used by all adapters; do not count decoded ciphertext bytes while storing larger base64 strings. Atomic admission rejects any single entry larger than the cap before insertion, eviction, revision allocation or receipt completion. Existing wire/batch ingress limits still apply. Evict oldest payloads until both bounds hold; preserve source-envelope identity/body hash fences so replay cannot become a new entry. Fences are not retained payload entries and require separate lifetime/accounting.
- Keep bounded active history without idle expiry. Freeze expiry at the **first canonical terminal instant + 10 minutes**, including accepted Host cancellation, abandonment and retirement. No read, replay or late append renews it. Content redaction/archive wins earlier. Completed task progress admission closes at the same atomic boundary; late envelopes have explicit existing/refined refusal behavior, never a newly visible event after a stream has drained and closed.
- Approval timeline: 50 retained observations / 1 MiB, same terminal-relative expiry. Never inactivity-expire a request that can still be decided. Never evict the unresolved request or the identity needed to resolve/replay its decision safely. Trim completed history; reject atomically if an incoming observation cannot fit without violating the invariant. Pending approval resolution and mailbox-control dedup remain one atomic operation. When a task becomes terminal, approval decision admission is closed consistently before cleanup can delete its request.
- **O5:** preserve the existing union variants (`state`, `agent`, `artifact`, `await_approval`, `error`), including `from: 'state'` intent. This is not a promise to replay every historical state transition: state is a coalesced projection of current durable task facts, not a new state journal. Error output is derived from the canonical failure result. Plaintext artifact order is log order. Opaque sealed progress must remain accessible through cloud reads; any stream exposed to sealed-capable compositions must carry it explicitly without decrypting or dropping it. Define that additive opaque-output variant in the shared contract tests; do not enable sealed dispatch in embedded topology as a side effect.
- Use a versioned tenant/task-bound composite cursor: log revision plus within-batch offset, approval revision, and canonical state observation identity. Cursor updates advance only the component actually yielded, so stopping halfway through a batch cannot lose the remainder. Define a deterministic ordering for a consistent read window (coalesced state, pending approval, log entries in revision order; terminal state/error only after the final admitted log window is drained). Do not imply a total historical causal order across sources that never stored one.
- Subscribe before the first read. Read a consistent source snapshot and generation/watermark, drain it, then recheck before sleeping. Invalidations are content-free hints; log/approval/task commits on another instance must wake the subscriber. Listener disconnect/reconnect forces an authoritative rescan; silent lost hints require a bounded liveness mechanism against the **same** authority, not relay fallback. Cursor below a retained floor or from the pre-cutover generation produces an explicit typed gap. Malformed or wrong-task cursors are invalid; neither silently resets. Retain enough floor/generation metadata to distinguish empty, expired and erased history without retaining removed content.
- **O6:** stop writers/readers, create/activate the new representation with a new generation, reject old cursors with a gap, document that no pre-cutover history is carried, then drop `activity_tail`. No row conversion, legacy parser, dual read, dual write or adopt-on-read. Existing tasks receive explicit empty/new-generation history, not fabricated history. Task/approval facts remain their own sources. Existing terminal tasks must not gain another ten minutes at deployment.
- **O7:** #1045 items 1–5, 7 and 8 remain in #1045 unchanged. Item 6 (artifact names/related paths and allowed metadata) gates sealed artifacts entering this log. Existing C3 progress encryption is reused. Log/read metadata must conform to #654's allowed set; no plaintext content in wake messages, logs, metrics or errors. This work does not certify that all other #1045 channels are encrypted.

Tenant/task admission quotas and retirement are therefore required operational bounds; a per-task cap is not a total-storage guarantee. Measure and report the first observed bottleneck, not an untested throughput promise.
5. Check #1172 is present, its removed endpoint cannot append, and operator external-caller inventory is complete before release. Check #1045 item 6 remains an explicit sealed-artifact gate. Do not mark all of #1113 complete merely because O4 or the store tests passed.
