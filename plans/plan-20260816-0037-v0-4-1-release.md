## Approach
### Strategy
Prepare one release PR that advances the aligned dispatch train from 0.4.0 to 0.4.1, updates the release tooling and CHANGELOG, and proves package bytes locally. Merge only after CI. Publish exactly the frozen merged-SHA tarballs and bind registry readback to their recorded integrity.

### Trade-offs
| Option | Pros | Cons | Decision |
|--------|------|------|----------|
| Publish only changed packages | Fewer registry writes | Splits an intentionally aligned 0.x train and leaves umbrella/dependency metadata ambiguous | Reject |
| Publish all eight aligned packages at 0.4.1 | One coherent downstream pin and rollback boundary | Eight immutable registry writes | Accept |
| Publish before PR merge | Fast | Tarballs cannot bind to the reviewed merged SHA | Reject |

## Risk Assessment
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|------------|
| Partial npm publication | Medium | High | Preflight ownership/version availability; publish dependencies before consumers; read back each immutable package before continuing |
| Published bytes differ from reviewed bytes | Low | Critical | Freeze once from exact merged SHA and compare registry `dist.integrity` to manifest SHA-512 |
| Split package train | Medium | High | Advance and validate all eight aligned packages; leave keys unchanged |
| Web auth expires mid-publish | Medium | Medium | Trigger only at final boundary; preserve manifest and resume from registry readback without repacking |
